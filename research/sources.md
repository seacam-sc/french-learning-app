## FEI-DELF

- **Title:** DELF tout public (A1, A2, B1, B2), exam descriptions and sample papers ("exemples de sujets")
- **Publisher:** France Éducation international (FEI), on behalf of the French Ministry of Education (wording from search results; not confirmed on the official page)
- **Year:** UNCONFIRMED (current format; the search summary mentions a new listening-test format introduced progressively, with a stated 2024–2025 timeline, but this was not verified)
- **URL(s):**
  - https://www.france-education-international.fr/ (official FEI domain; NOT reachable from this session)
  - https://france-education-international.fr/document/kit-evolutions-dd (appeared as a search-result link; the "kit évolutions" document. NOT fetched.)
  - No official URL for the DELF tout public sample papers was confirmed. The FEI page for sample papers was not reached.
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-10-16
- **Type:** exam specification
- **Contents summary:** DELF tout public has four tests: collective listening, collective reading, collective writing and an individual oral test. Each test is marked out of 25, and a candidate passes with at least 50/100 and at least 5/25 per test (from search summaries). Stated durations conflict between sources. A CASNAV Lille school booklet gives collective listening 20/25/25 min, reading 30/30/45 min, writing 30/45/45 min, and oral 5–7/6–8/15 min for A1/A2/B1 (preparation time additional). A commercial prep site gives B2 listening as 30 min. Do NOT use these durations until checked against the FEI official specification.
- **Reuse terms (sample papers):** Not stated / UNCONFIRMED. The official sample papers were not reached.
- **Confidence:** partly confirmed (the four-test structure and pass rule are corroborated across search summaries; official URLs, sample-paper location, durations and reuse terms are not confirmed)
